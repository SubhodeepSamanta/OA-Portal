## Section 1 — Mathematical Ability

**Q1 — (A) ₹1,000.** 1331/1000 = 1.331 = (1.1)³.

**Q2 — (C) 24 days.** Total 360 man-days. 8 days × 15 = 120 done → 240 left. 10 men → 24 days.

**Q3 — (B) 20.5.** (n+1)/2 = 41/2.

**Q4 — (B) 48 km/h.** Equal distances → 2ab/(a+b) = 2(40)(60)/100.

**Q5 — (A) 115.** 2W2M: 6 × 15 = 90 · 3W1M: 4 × 6 = 24 · 4W: 1 → 90 + 24 + 1 = 115.

**Q6 — (A) 198.** k³ − 3k = 216 − 18.

**Q7 — (D) 27.** Let the numbers be 3k and 5k.  
(3k + 9)/(5k + 9) = 2/3 → 9k + 27 = 10k + 18 → k = 9.  
Smaller = 3k = **27**. *(Check: 36/54 = 2/3 ✓)*

**Q8 — (A) 1/3.** ⁶C₂/¹⁰C₂ = 15/45.

**Q9 — (B) 32.** 1080 = 2³ × 3³ × 5 → 4 × 4 × 2.

**Q10 — (B) loses 1%.** Always y²/100 = 100/100 = 1% loss.

## Section 2 — Logical Reasoning

**Q11 — (C) 95.** ×2 + 1.

**Q12 — (A) Only I.** The non-dog cats need not be animals.

**Q13 — (A) PQRABKQ.** Each letter −3, wrapping past A.

**Q14 — (B) Aunt.** C is A and B's father; D is C's sister.

**Q15 — (B) 29.** 40 − 12 + 1.

**Q16 — (C) Both together.** Divisible by 3 and 4 → divisible by 12.

**Q17 — (A) 3 km east.** Facing south, left is east; the two 5 km legs cancel.

**Q18 — (C) Mango.** The rest are flowers.

**Q19 — (A) Only I.** II is a course of action, not an assumption, and it is extreme.

**Q20 — (B) 216.** 8 = 2³ gives 512 = 8³; the relation is the cube → 6³.

## Section 3 — Verbal Ability

**Q21 — (B) when.** *Hardly … when*.

**Q22 — (B).** "Each of" is singular → **has submitted**.

**Q23 — (B) frank.**

**Q24 — (C) flimsy.** An acquittal follows weak evidence.

**Q25 — (B).** *Prefer X to Y*.

**Q26 — (B) something bad that turns out well.**

**Q27 — (C).** Subject is *quality*, singular → **is far superior**.

**Q28 — (A) QPRS.** Q introduces the cause, P the effect, R contrasts with "Others", S expands on R.

## Section 4 — Pseudocode

**Q29 — (B) 3, 2.** Integer division truncates.

**Q30 — (C) 30.** 1 + 4 + 9 + 16.

**Q31 — (A) 6, 10.** `++x` makes x = 5 and yields 5; `x++` yields 5 then makes x = 6. 5 + 5 = 10.

**Q32 — (A) 6.** One digit, so the reverse is itself.

**Q33 — (B) 48.** 6 × 4 × 2 × f(0) = 6 × 4 × 2 × 1.

**Q34 — (A) 18.** i = 0, 2, 4 → 2 + 6 + 10.

**Q35 — (B) no.** Short-circuit: `a != 0` is false, so the division never runs.

**Q36 — (B) 7.** i = 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64.

**Q37 — (C) O(n²).** Nested loops dominate; sequential parts add.

**Q38 — (B) 10.** 4 + 3 + 2 + 1.

## Section 5 — Puzzle Solving

**Q39 — (A) 17 min.** 1&2 cross (2), 1 back (1), 5&10 cross (10), 2 back (2), 1&2 cross (2).  
Sending the two slowest together is the key; escorting with the fastest gives 19.

**Q40 — (C).** Rope A at both ends burns in 30 minutes, leaving 30 on rope B; lighting B's second end burns the rest in 15. 30 + 15 = 45.

**Q41 — (B) 28.** The third column is the product of the first two: 6 × 3 = 18, 8 × 5 = 40,  
7 × 4 = 28.

**Q42 — (B) 2.** Split 3, 3, 2. Each weighing has three outcomes, so two weighings cover up to 9 balls.
