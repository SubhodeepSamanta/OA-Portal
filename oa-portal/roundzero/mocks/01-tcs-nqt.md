## Section 1 — Numerical Ability

**Q1 — (B) 17%.** CP = 100 → MP = 130 → SP = 130 × 0.9 = 117 → 17% profit.

**Q2 — (C) 7.** Cycle of 7 is (7,9,3,1), length 4. 85 ÷ 4 leaves remainder 1 → first entry.

**Q3 — (B) 30 days.** Work = LCM(12,20) = 60. Combined 5/day, A = 3/day → B = 2/day → 60/2.

**Q4 — (B) 22.5 s.** 72 km/h = 20 m/s. Distance = 200 + 250 = 450 → 450/20.

**Q5 — (A) 56.** (49 × 6) + (52 × 6) − (50 × 11) = 294 + 312 − 550 = 56.

**Q6 — (C) ₹5,000.** P(R/100)² = 50 → P × 0.01 = 50.

**Q7 — (A) 1 : 7.** Water is free, so by alligation on cost: (32 − 28) : (28 − 0) = 4 : 28 = 1 : 7.

**Q8 — (C) 14.** ⌊60/5⌋ + ⌊60/25⌋ = 12 + 2.

**Q9 — (B) 1/12.** Sum 10 has 3 ways: (4,6)(5,5)(6,4) → 3/36.

**Q10 — (C) 4% decrease.** 20 − 20 − 400/100 = −4.

**Q11 — (B) 20.** (5k+4)/(7k+4) = 3/4 → 20k + 16 = 21k + 12 → k = 4 → A = 20.

**Q12 — (B) 2 km/h.** Downstream 8, upstream 4 → stream = (8−4)/2.

**Q13 — (A) 30.** LEVEL has 5 letters with L×2 and E×2 → 5!/(2!2!) = 120/4.

**Q14 — (D) 8 times.** Volume scales by k³ = 2³.

**Q15 — (A) 352.** 5000 ÷ 25 = 200; 19% of 800 ≈ 152 → 352.

## Section 2 — Verbal Ability

**Q16 — (B) has.** Subject is *list*, singular.

**Q17 — (C).** Neither/nor agrees with the nearer subject, *coach* → **was present**.

**Q18 — (B) thorough.**

**Q19 — (C) scarce.**

**Q20 — (B) sceptical.** *Although* signals contrast with "attractive".

**Q21 — (B).** *Senior* takes **to**, not *than*.

**Q22 — (B) accommodation.** Double c, double m.

**Q23 — (C) since.** A point in time.

**Q24 — (B) to face a difficulty bravely.**

**Q25 — (A).** "One of my **friends**" — plural noun.

**Q26 — (C) than.** *No sooner … than*; *hardly … when*.

**Q27 — (B) atheist.** An *agnostic* holds it unknowable, which is different.

**Q28 — (A) QSPR.** Q introduces EVs, S adds a second benefit, P turns with "However", R gives the obstacle.

**Q29 — (B).** Collective noun takes a singular verb in this usage.

**Q30 — (C) Presenting both sides.** The passage gives the critics' case and the defenders' reply without endorsing either.

## Section 3 — Reasoning Ability

**Q31 — (C) 63.** Each term is ×2 + 1.

**Q32 — (A) DBOEMF.** Each letter +1.

**Q33 — (C) Mother.** "The only daughter of my mother" is the speaker herself, and she is his mother.

**Q34 — (A) 5 km.** √(16 + 9) — a 3-4-5 triple.

**Q35 — (C) Both follow.** All+All gives All; and "all pens are papers" converts to "some papers are pens".

**Q36 — (C) 200.** The rest are 11², 12², 13², 14².

**Q37 — (B) Saw.** The relationship is worker to characteristic tool.

**Q38 — (C) Both together.** I alone gives x = ±7; II alone gives nothing; together x = 7.

**Q39 — (B) 12.** 12(n−2) with n = 3.

**Q40 — (C) P.** Positions 2, 4, 7, 11 — differences +2, +3, +4 → next +5 = 16 = P.

**Q41 — (C) 3.** Opposite faces sum to 7.

**Q42 — (D) Cannot be determined.** Only A, B and D are placed; the seat to A's left could be  
C, E or F.

**Q43 — (A) Only I follows.** II is disproportionate.

**Q44 — (B) na.** The two sentences share the word *sweet* and share the code *na*.

**Q45 — (C) 24.** It computes 4! = 4 × 3 × 2.
