# Mock 3 — Wipro speed drill

Wipro gives roughly **one minute per question** with no going back. This paper is built to break you on time, not on difficulty — every question here is easy in isolation.

**That is the entire point.** If you score badly, the fix is pace and shortcut recall, not more theory.

## Before you start

| Section | Questions | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Quantitative | 12 | **12 min** |
| Logical Reasoning | 10 | **10 min** |
| Verbal | 12 | **12 min** |

Set a hard timer. **When it expires, stop mid-question.** Then note how many you did not reach — that number is the real result of this mock.

**No negative marking. Fill every blank before time expires, even blindly.**

# Answer key

## Section 1 — Quantitative

**Q1 — (A) 288.** 45% = 9/20 → 640 × 9/20.  
**Q2 — (B) 36.** 37.5% = 3/8 → 96 × 3/8.  
**Q3 — (B) 40%.** 20 + 25 − 5 = 40. Or ×4/5 × ×3/4 = ×3/5.  
**Q4 — (C) 16 days.** 8 × 12 = 96 man-days ÷ 6.  
**Q5 — (B) 8 s.** 54 km/h = 15 m/s → 120/15.  
**Q6 — (B) 16.** 12, 14, 16, 18, 20 — the middle term.  
**Q7 — (B) ₹1,200.** 5000 × 8 × 3/100.  
**Q8 — (B) 4225.** 6 × 7 = 42, append 25.  
**Q9 — (A) 3 : 5.** 75 : 125.  
**Q10 — (B) 14.3%.** T = 100/R → R = 100/7.  
**Q11 — (B) 3.** Cycle (3,9,7,1); 33 mod 4 = 1 → first entry.  
**Q12 — (B) 200.** 5000 ÷ 25.

## Section 2 — Logical Reasoning

**Q13 — (B) 42.** n(n+1) → 6 × 7.  
**Q14 — (A) GSJEBZ.** Each letter +1.  
**Q15 — (C) 14.** The only non-prime.  
**Q16 — (B) 17.** 25 − 9 + 1.  
**Q17 — (C) Grandmother.** C is B's mother, B is A's parent.  
**Q18 — (B) R.** 26, 24, 22, 20 → 18 = R.  
**Q19 — (B) Cut.** Object to its function.  
**Q20 — (A) 10 km.** √(36 + 64) — a 6-8-10 triple.  
**Q21 — (B) Does not follow.** All + Some yields nothing; the fading flowers need not be roses.  
**Q22 — (A) 40.** The rule is **(a × b) − b**.  
Check: 15 − 3 = 12 ✓ and 32 − 4 = 28 ✓ → 45 − 5 = **40**

## Section 3 — Verbal

**Q23 — (B) is.** "**The** number of" is singular.  
**Q24 — (B) clear.**  
**Q25 — (B) arrogant.**  
**Q26 — (B) to.**  
**Q27 — (B).** Collective noun as one body → **is playing**.  
**Q28 — (B) necessary.** One c, two s.  
**Q29 — (C) for.** A duration.  
**Q30 — (B) very rarely.**  
**Q31 — (B).** *Neither* is singular.  
**Q32 — (B) illegible.**  
**Q33 — (C) working.** *Despite* takes a gerund.  
**Q34 — (A).** *Returned back* is redundant.

## Scoring — read this differently from the other mocks

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Questions not reached** | your pace problem — the main thing this mock measures |
| **Wrong answers among those attempted** | your accuracy problem |

If you reached everything with a few errors, you are in good shape for Wipro. If you left five or more unreached, **pace is your bottleneck** and more theory will not help.

**The three fixes for pace, in order of effect:**

1. **Memorise the fraction ↔ percentage table.** Q1, Q2 and Q12 are all instant with it and
   slow without it.
2. **Do a first pass taking only the easy ones.** Never answer strictly in order.
3. **Enforce the 60-second rule.** Guess and move. A blank scores zero; a guess scores 0.25
   on average.
